For his YouTube/Short edits use the `talking-head-screen-edit` skill (built Oct 2026 on the Google TV debloat video). Related: [[recipe-reel-preferences]].

- Tech videos: he is in control and knowledgeable; never "I let AI do it" or "experiment". No "should I try it on my phone / comment below" CTA.
- Motion graphics (overlays + full-screen), cut-paper style but colourful with tech props and Space Grotesk / JetBrains Mono; he hated a dark templated card look and homely props (cats, clocks).
- PiP face card centred on the right; screen recording rounded, menu bar cropped; TV shots full-frame with punch-in; face always centred.
- Grade: lift shadows, gentle contrast/saturation (dark skin patches get amplified otherwise).
- VO lines replace weaker audio without lengthening; joins use his own connector words.
- Full quality only; render on his external drive "Navy2TB" when the Mac is short on space.

**Why:** all stated or corrected by him during the TV video (he rejected the first graphics set, the homely props, and the "AI did it" framing).
**How to apply:** start from these defaults, show an edit plan + graphic stills + contact sheet before rendering, and look at frames yourself before showing him.
